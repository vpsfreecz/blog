We keep a close eye on our membership growth, and we're pleased to see the numbers keep rising.

We're growing by 20 to 30% year on year. At least, that's what the statistics we recently compiled show; we plan to update them every six months. Three years ago, we had 380 members. Today, we're approaching nine hundred.

As you can see, the trend is very good, and growth is actually speeding up. We're happy about that: it means our project makes sense and is reaching more and more users.
