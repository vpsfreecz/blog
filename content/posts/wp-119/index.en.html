Just as last year, we cannot miss the biggest Czech Linux event. We are going to LinuxDays.

As last year, we are going to LinuxDays, the biggest Czech Linux conference. We plan to have a stand again, staffed in turn by our members. You can talk to them about vpsFree.cz and their experience running Linux servers and various services.

You will find two talks from us in the program: Jakub Jelen will discuss the current state of SSH, and David Karban will talk about automating server administration with Ansible.

You can find photos from the previous year in our photo gallery. We are looking forward to seeing you. Register and come and see us, or even join our community on the spot.

Come to LinuxDays. We will definitely be there!
