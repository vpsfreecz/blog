We're heading to LinuxDays this weekend, bringing a stand, a server, speakers and great laptop stickers.

What are you doing this weekend? On 6 and 7 October, we'll be at the LinuxDays conference, held as usual at FIT ČVUT in Dejvice. Expect the Linux regulars, excellent coffee and, of course, our stand. Entry is free, but don't forget to register.

We're giving talks again this year: Pavel Šnajdr will discuss developing our own hardware for vpsFree.cz, then join Richard Marko to introduce NixOS. Together, they'll also describe developing the vpsAdminOS virtualization platform. Petr Krčmář will talk about network interface naming in Linux and the Syncthing synchronization tool. Věroš Kaplan will cover the Restic backup tool, and Ondřej Caletka will discuss managing zone files in Git. Those are only some of the speakers who are vpsFree.cz members.

As usual, you'll also find us at our stand, handing out chocolates, showing a server and demonstrating our new vpsAdminOS virtualization platform. Above all, you can chat with us about vpsFree.cz and virtualization, as well as other Linux topics. You can also take home a nice hexagonal laptop sticker.
