We're packing the car, loading the last things and heading to InstallFest in Prague. Come and see us.

It's spring, which means InstallFest is here! We're heading into the bustle of the city again to set up our stand at the traditional Linux conference. Catch a train, a bus or your dragon, and don't miss it.

We'll have our vpsFree stand again, where we'll make you excellent coffee. While you drink it, chat with us about virtualization, servers, Linux, open source and plenty of other things.

You'll see our new switch, which will soon carry all traffic between individual nodes (servers). We'll also show one of our servers, of which we have plenty in the datacentre. Come along: it's on Saturday and Sunday in the ČVUT building at Karlovo náměstí.

If you can't wait, look at last year's photos.
