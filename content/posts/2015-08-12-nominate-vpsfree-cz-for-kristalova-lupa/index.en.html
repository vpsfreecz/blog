Nominations are open for this year's Křišťálová Lupa award. We'd like to take part, and you can help!

Křišťálová Lupa recognizes interesting Czech projects every year, and we think we have a chance of making it onto the ballot. We're building a unique project that aims to reach the wider IT community. Help us!

It's simple:

  1. visit the nomination form
  2. enter www.vpsfree.cz in the "Nástroje a služby" (Tools and services) category
  3. fill in your email address at the bottom and submit

Many thanks to everyone who submits a nomination!
