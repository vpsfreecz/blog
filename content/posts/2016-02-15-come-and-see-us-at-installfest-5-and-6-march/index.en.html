The eighth annual InstallFest conference takes place at Strahov on the first weekend in March. Once again, we will be there.

Put the weekend of 5 and 6 March in your calendar: the next InstallFest will be held at Strahov in Prague. We were there last year, and we are coming again this year.

Pavel Šnajdr and Jakub Skokan will talk about HaveAPI, which powers our vpsAdmin. Need to put together a new API quickly? A little Ruby code will create the API along with documentation, bindings for many languages, and access through an HTTP REST API and a console utility. The talk will include creating a GPIO API on a Raspberry Pi.

Petr Krčmář will talk about Let's Encrypt, which offers free HTTPS certificates to everyone. He will explain how it works, discuss the latest developments and introduce a range of clients you can use to obtain a certificate.

We will also provide excellent coffee again, an essential part of a pleasant conference. Do come along. We will meet by the coffee machine and talk about more than just vpsFree.cz.
