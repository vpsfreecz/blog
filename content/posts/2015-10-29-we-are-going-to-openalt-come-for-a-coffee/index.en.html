The annual OpenAlt conference (formerly LinuxAlt) takes place in Brno on the weekend of 7 and 8 November. We will be there. Come and have a chat.

After a very successful LinuxDays, which we could not miss, we are heading to OpenAlt in Brno. Do come along. Once again, there will be plenty of talks, stands and, above all, interesting people. You can talk to us about more than just Linux on servers and our VPSes.

Petr Krčmář will speak for us at OpenAlt about installing Debian with debootstrap. You could use it to install your own system on a vpsFree.cz virtual server without using a prepared template. There will certainly be a practical demonstration.

We have also prepared a workshop on the basics of administering a Linux VPS. If you are considering your own server but are afraid to jump in, visit the practical workshop, where Pavel Šnajdr will show you that there is nothing to be frightened of.

We will also have our stand again (as last year), where we will show you a server, explain everything important about our association and demonstrate a cutting plotter (we will be cutting laptop stickers). Alongside all that, the baristas from Penerini Coffee will make you excellent coffee. Get on a train and come along!
