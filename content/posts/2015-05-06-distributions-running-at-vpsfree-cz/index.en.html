A look at Linux distributions and their share of our VPSes. Debian and Ubuntu lead by a clear margin, running on three quarters of our VPSes.

At conferences, people quite often ask which distributions we offer and what proportion of our VPSes run each one. So I put together some statistics based on the installed templates. Here are the results:

Distribution Share Count
Arch Linux 1.3% 15
CentOS 10.1% 112
Debian 53.3% 593
Fedora 1.2% 13
Gentoo 3.8% 42
OpenSUSE 0.5% 6
Scientific 7.8% 87
Ubuntu 21.9% 244

As you can see, Debian leads by a clear margin, followed some way behind by Ubuntu. Together, these two deb distributions are used by 75% of our users. The rpm distributions CentOS, Fedora and Scientific also have a substantial share, together accounting for about 19%. The other distributions divide up the remainder.
