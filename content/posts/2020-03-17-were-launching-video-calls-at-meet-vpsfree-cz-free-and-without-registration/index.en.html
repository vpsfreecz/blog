We're launching a publicly available video-calling platform for meetings, teaching, virtual gatherings and whatever else is needed.

The current situation requires people to meet in person as little as possible, creating demand for videoconferencing services. We've decided to offer an open solution using our infrastructure. You can make calls now, with no special tools: just a web browser.

Open meet.vpsfree.cz, choose a meeting room name and enter it. When the other participants do the same, you have a video conference and can talk. It's that easy: no registration, payment or anything else.

Before using it, please check your microphone settings, particularly the volume. An unnecessarily high microphone gain creates noise in the room and startles other users.

You can lock the room with a password, share your screen or just use text chat. It's up to you. All communication is encrypted; we collect no data about it and don't feed it to multinational corporations.

Mobile apps are available (App Store and Google Play). In settings, select meet.vpsfree.cz as the server and optionally choose a nickname. The rest works just like the website.

If you'd like a similar videoconferencing solution on your own infrastructure, it's an installation of Jitsi Meet.
