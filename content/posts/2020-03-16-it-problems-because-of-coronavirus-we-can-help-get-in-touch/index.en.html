Are you having problems related to coronavirus? We are a strong community of specialists and are offering a helping hand. Do not hesitate to get in touch.

The current situation surrounding the coronavirus pandemic is very difficult. Government measures complicate daily life as well as work.

Those of us in IT can often sit at home and work in isolation, without direct contact with colleagues. That also puts us in a position to help one another with the various problems we face. As the vpsFree.cz community, we have plenty of specialist knowledge that we would like to share with those who need it.

We have also agreed to provide free hosting for projects addressing something related to COVID-19 that will benefit us all.

Our active team is available on the IRC channel and ready to help resolve problems.

You can also use our mailing lists to ask a question or request IT help. Write to our community list. There are hundreds of bright people there who can help.

We can help with monitoring, backups, infrastructure outages, various technical problems and more. The situation is difficult, but we can get through it by helping one another.
