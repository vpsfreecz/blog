We were at InstallFest over the weekend, showing our work-in-progress project vpsAdminOS.

The tenth InstallFest took place over the weekend, and we couldn't miss it. We had a stand, made excellent coffee and talked with visitors about our vpsAdminOS project. It's a small Linux operating system based on NixOS, providing a virtualization platform for virtual servers in containers.

It's still under active development, and we'll be integrating it into vpsAdmin. In three or four months, we expect to begin experimental deployments, gradually replacing the declining OpenVZ. Pavel Šnajdr also gave a short talk on it, which you can already watch thanks to the excellent work of AVC SH. The talk slides [PDF] are available too.
