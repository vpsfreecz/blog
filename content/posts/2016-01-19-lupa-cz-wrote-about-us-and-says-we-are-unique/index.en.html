Lupa.cz wrote about us today, with a lovely explanation of the idea behind our association and the new things we are preparing.

The association is growing promisingly. vpsFree currently has more than 900 members running around 1200 virtual machines on twenty physical servers. "We are growing by tens of percent a year. This year, we should pass a thousand members," Šnajdr tells Lupa. "I believe we have the potential to grow to three or four times our current size." A Czech project operating as a community on this scale appears to be unique worldwide.
