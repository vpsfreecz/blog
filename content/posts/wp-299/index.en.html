OSS Víkend took place in Bratislava on the weekend of 9 and 10 April, where Tomáš Srnka spoke about the past of vpsFree.cz and more.

In his OSS Víkend talk, Tomáš Srnka looked back at how vpsFree.cz began eight years ago. Today, the project has almost a thousand members.

He tells the story of offering his own server to Pavel Šnajdr, expecting never to see it again. That wasn't how it turned out: vpsFree.cz started growing very quickly and is still doing well today. The whole talk was recorded on video, so you can watch it:
