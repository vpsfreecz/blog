Where do vpsFree.cz members come from? We've put together detailed statistics, including maps.

We wanted to know where vpsFree.cz members actually come from, and whether the internet really breaks down geographical boundaries. It turns out it does: our members come from all corners of our two countries.

The figures come from a slightly older database (we're growing very quickly). At that point, we had 786 members in 322 different towns and cities, averaging two and a half members per place. In practice, though, the distribution is far from even. Here are the seven cities where we have at least ten members:

Number of members City
188 Praha
58 Brno
47 Bratislava
14 Liberec
12 Olomouc
11 Plzeň
10 Žilina

As you can see, almost half of all members live in these seven cities. At the other end, we have exactly one member in each of 252 municipalities. The map shows the distribution nicely:

The project's current leadership is just as decentralized, with members in Brno, Bratislava, Děčín, Liberec, Ostrava and Prague. We naturally handle most business online, but also manage to meet regularly. You can join us at the next meeting too.
